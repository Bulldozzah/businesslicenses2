eRegistry
========================

The eRegistry is an online portal of business licenses issued by government agencies and local authorities. The purpose of the eRegistry is to provide easy access to exhaustive information about business licenses and the formalities businesses must comply with to obtain the licenses. The registry is a one stop shop for all licensing information required to engage in activities or set up businesses in a country and different administrative jurisdictions.

The eRegistry also provides an intuitive and user friendly interface that allows the system administrators to easily manage the licenses contained in the eRegistry.
